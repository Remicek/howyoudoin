Tired of jumping between WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram and a handful of other chat apps? HowYouDoin is an all-in-one messenger that puts every conversation in one unified inbox, so you can read and reply to everyone from a single app.

It works with WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, Signal, Discord, Slack, LinkedIn, Bluesky, X and Matrix. Connect the networks you use and your chats show up together, newest first.

ONE INBOX FOR ALL YOUR MESSAGES
• Every chat from every network in one list
• Filter by network with a tap, or show only unread chats
• Search your chats, and start a new chat with anyone on a connected network
• Notifications for new messages without push servers: the app keeps its own connection

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your accounts connect directly from this phone. There are no HowYouDoin servers in the middle of your messages, and nobody else sees them
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Lock the app with your fingerprint or a PIN, and hide message text in notifications
• Crash reports stay on your phone unless you choose to share them

A FULL CHAT APP
• Text, photos, videos, voice messages and files
• Replies, reactions, edits, deletions, typing indicators and read receipts, wherever the network supports them
• Voice and video calls with Matrix contacts
• Offline? Your saved chats stay readable, and the app tells you when a message can't be sent yet

EASY TO START
• WhatsApp, Telegram and Signal link like their desktop apps, with a QR code or a pairing code
• Messenger, Instagram, Slack, LinkedIn, Discord and X sign in through their own websites inside the app
• A short tour shows you around the inbox
• Try everything in demo mode first, without connecting an account
• If a network stops working, the app tells you and shows how to fix it

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light and dark mode and four colour themes
• Available in 12 languages

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
No subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Licensed under the GNU AGPL v3; the source code is on GitHub.
